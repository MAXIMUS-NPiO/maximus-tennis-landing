## Commit signing

From 1 October 2026 every commit published to `main` is signed with an ed25519 key held only on the author's own computer, and GitHub shows it as Verified. Commits made before that date are unsigned and are left as they are: rewriting published history to obtain a badge would destroy the dates that give this record its value.
